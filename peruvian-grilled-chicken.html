Peruvian-style grilled chicken

Boneless, skinless thighs in a soy, lime, cumin and smoked paprika marinade, grilled hot and fast over the coals, 6 minutes a side, off at 175°F and rested. A mild green herb sauce in the aji verde style, a big salad on the house vinaigrette, rice for the boys. Set the time you want to eat and everything runs itself backward.

Notes

Mild for the boys: no chile in the marinade or the table sauce. His heat is the jalapeno blended into a third of the green sauce, kept in its own bowl.

Marinade time: 2 to 4 hours in the fridge. Past about 4 hours the lime starts to turn the meat mealy.

Food safety: chicken to 165°F minimum on a probe in the thickest part. These come off at 175°F, which is above the minimum and keeps thighs tender, then rest 5 minutes. Never judge by the juices. The used marinade goes down the drain, never on the food.

Salt: the kosher salt amounts are sized for Diamond Crystal. Morton is about twice as salty by volume: use half.

Infrared readings: the gun reads only the surface it points at, never the inside of the food or the depth of a liquid. Cast iron and dark, seasoned pans read true; shiny stainless and aluminum read low, so aim at the oil, the fat or a dark spot. Aim close, since the spot it reads grows with distance, and read two or three spots: pans vary a lot from edge to centre. On a grill, aim at a grate bar, not the coals. Simmering water, brine and deep oil get a probe thermometer instead. Oven and smoker numbers are the dial settings.
