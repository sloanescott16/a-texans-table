## The four recipes, settled for 4 (stage 2 builds the pages from these)

Sun 25, Peruvian-style grilled chicken, aji verde style sauce, big salad, rice for the boys
- Chicken: 2.5 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs. Marinade: 3 tbsp soy sauce, 3 tbsp lime juice, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp jarred minced garlic, 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp brown sugar, 2 tsp kosher salt, 1 tsp black pepper. Grilled direct, 6 minutes a side, pulled at 175°F on a probe (165°F minimum), 5 minute rest.
- Green sauce, mild: 2 cups packed cilantro (about 1 bunch), 1/2 cup mayonnaise, 1/4 cup crumbled cotija, 2 tbsp lime juice, 1 tsp jarred minced garlic, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1/4 tsp kosher salt. His side: blend 1 jalapeno into a third of it.
- Salad: 2 romaine hearts, 1 pint cherry tomatoes, 1 cucumber, half a red onion, 1 avocado. Base vinaigrette: 2 tbsp cider vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon, 6 tbsp olive oil, 1/2 tsp kosher salt, 1/4 tsp black pepper.
- Rice for the boys: 1 cup jasmine rice.

Mon 26, carne guisada, Instant Pot on slow cook, rice cooker at 5, tortillas
- 2.5 lb beef chuck roast in 1.5 inch cubes, 1 tbsp beef tallow, 2 tbsp all-purpose flour, 1 yellow onion, 1 green bell pepper, 1 tbsp jarred minced garlic, 1 tbsp chili powder, 2 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 cup tomato sauce, 1.5 cups beef stock, 2 bay leaves, 2 tsp kosher salt, 1 tsp black pepper.
- 2 cups jasmine rice in the rice cooker. 8 flour tortillas. Bowl toppings: chopped cilantro, diced red onion. His heat: 1 sliced jalapeno.

Wed 28, tom kha, her recipe (40 Aprons Slow Cooker Tom Kha Soup), Instant Pot on slow cook, as published (research\tom-kha-40aprons-1024.md)
- Broth, High 3 hours: about 1 cup sliced white onion, 1 tbsp chopped garlic (jarred minced), 3 slices galangal (or ginger), 1 stalk lemongrass, 10 lime leaves torn (optional), 1 tbsp red Thai curry paste (can be halved if the boys find it warm), 4 cups chicken stock. Strain.
- Then, High about 1 hour to 165°F: 2 cans full-fat coconut milk, 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast in bite-sized pieces, 8 oz sliced white mushrooms.
- Finish: 2 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tbsp fish sauce, 2 tbsp lime juice, to taste. Garnish sliced green onions, cilantro.
- Mild: the half red jalapeno or 1 to 2 Thai chiles come out of the pot and go on the side for him, sliced, with chili oil.
- 1.5 cups jasmine rice for the boys. Start about 2:00 pm for 6:30.

Thu 29, cast iron pork chops, caramelized onion and pear pan sauce, mashed potatoes
- 4 bone-in pork chops about 1 inch thick, 2 tsp kosher salt, 1 tsp black pepper, 1 tbsp canola. To 145°F on a probe, 3 minute rest.
- Sauce: 2 large yellow onions sliced, 1 tbsp butter plus 2 tbsp to finish, 2 pears, 1 cup apple cider, 1/2 cup chicken stock, 4 sprigs fresh thyme, 1 tsp Dijon, 1 tsp cider vinegar, 1/2 tsp kosher salt.
- Mash from the site's mash steps at 3 lb: gold potatoes, 4 tbsp butter, 2 tbsp olive oil, 2 garlic cloves, 2 rosemary sprigs, 1 cup heavy cream, 1/2 tsp MSG, 1/2 cup Parmesan, kosher salt, bay leaf.

Stock on every page is made up at home: beef bouillon paste or chicken stock powder, 1 teaspoon to each cup of hot water.
