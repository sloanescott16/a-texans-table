Taco Thursday chicken tacos

Chicken thighs, dry brined since Tuesday, pressure cooked in salsa verde with onion, garlic, lime, oregano and one whole chipotle for smoke. Store run at 3:30, pot on at 4:15, and it holds on Keep Warm while you fetch the kids. At 6:00 the chicken is shredded into its juices, broiled for crisp edges, and the tortillas are charred on the burner. Mild for the boys, heat on the side for you. Set the time you want to eat and everything runs itself backward.

Notes

Done temperature: chicken thighs are done at 175°F or above in the thickest part. Pressure cooking takes them well past that, which is why they shred. Keep Warm holds them above 145°F while you are out, lid on. Probe at the end of the natural release, before you leave.

Where the flavour comes from: the brine since Tuesday seasons the meat right through. The salsa verde, onion, garlic, lime and oregano make the braise, the bay leaf rounds it, and one whole chipotle gives smoke. The broiler gives crisp brown edges, and the burner gives the tortillas a char.

Mild for the boys: the chipotle goes in whole and unbroken and comes out at 6:00, so it gives smoke, not heat. Do not split it or add the adobo sauce. The jalapenos and hot sauce are for your plate.

Burn warning: thin liquid on the bottom and no stirring after loading keeps it away. If it shows, Cancel, push the valve to Venting, scrape the bottom, add 1/4 cup more stock and start the 12 minutes again.

Size: up to 5 lb of thighs (8 servings) fits a 6 quart Instant Pot. The 12 minutes stay the same, but a fuller pot takes longer to come to pressure, so set Eating at 10 minutes later.
